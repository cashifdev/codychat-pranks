# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-22)
- First public release
- 16 built-in pranks in four tabs: Splat, Silly, Magic and Sweet, each with its own animation and sound
- One free "Bounce it back" for the person pranked, within 20 seconds
- Admin panel: add your own pranks with an emoji or a picture (PNG, GIF, WEBP, JPG), your own chat line, pop-up word, effect and .mp3 sound
- Give any built-in prank your own .mp3 sound, or switch it off
- Light and dark picker. Auto follows your chat theme
- Volume, cooldown, built-in sounds on/off and max sound size in the admin panel
- Members can mute prank sounds from the picker
- Respects "reduce motion" on the member's device
- Works on shared hosting: no WebSocket server and no Redis needed
- Optional credit line (on by default), which admins can turn off
